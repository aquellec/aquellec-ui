<h2 className="mt-10 mb-3 text-title font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-50">When to use, and when not</h2>

<div className="mt-4 grid gap-4 md:grid-cols-2">
  <div className="rounded-card border border-neutral-200 p-4 dark:border-neutral-700">
    <p className="text-label font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">Do</p>
    <ul className="mt-2 space-y-1.5 text-label text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-300">
      <li>rank columns by what a recruiter scans first — candidate, score, status — and hide the rest below <code>sm</code>;</li>
      <li>let a score's colour follow its tier, and keep the number readable on its own;</li>
      <li>give an empty table a sentence that says what would fill it, not “No data”;</li>
      <li>write a real threshold into ProgressBar rather than colouring at 80% out of habit.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div className="rounded-card border border-neutral-200 p-4 dark:border-neutral-700">
    <p className="text-label font-semibold text-neutral-900 dark:text-neutral-100">Do not</p>
    <ul className="mt-2 space-y-1.5 text-label text-neutral-600 dark:text-neutral-300">
      <li>use a gauge for two numbers side by side; a gauge is for one figure that carries a decision;</li>
      <li>expect DataTable to virtualise — it is built for paginated views, and says so;</li>
      <li>wrap a cell to avoid a scroll: a wrapped badge clips, and the table scrolls sideways on purpose;</li>
      <li>put more than one primary action in a card footer.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>
